How it Works

Four Steps to Safer Care

A quiet, continuous chain of sensing, data fusion, AI detection, and tiered alerts — completely invisible to residents, yet essential for the caregivers who depend on it.

radar
1
Sense
Advanced millimeter-wave radar silently sweeps the room, capturing precise motion, position, and micro-vibrations — day or night, without requiring any light.
hub
2
Fuse
Radar data fuses with infrared thermal sensing on the edge processor — two independent modalities combined for significantly higher detection confidence and far fewer false alarms.
psychology
3
Detect
Embedded AI classifies falls, bed exits, abnormal breathing, and extended inactivity in milliseconds — learning from patterns over time to become more accurate with each passing day.
notification_important
4
Alert
Caregivers receive instant, prioritized notifications through the eLumino app — with enough situational context to respond calmly and confidently, without the anxiety of ambiguity.
Features

Complete Peace of Mind

Clinical-grade reliability in home and care settings — without making any room feel clinical or intrusive.

falling
Fall Detection
Continuous positional sensing detects rapid elevation changes even in complete darkness. Multi-stage event filtering eliminates false positives so caregivers receive only meaningful, actionable alerts.
bed
Bed-Exit Alerts
Monitors bed-transition patterns to detect night-time wandering risk before it becomes dangerous. Sensitivity and timing thresholds are fully configurable per resident — and per time of day.
air
Breathing Monitoring
Continuous detection of chest micro-movements provides real-time respiratory rate tracking, with automatic anomaly alerts for irregular breathing patterns or potential distress — even during sleep.
thermostat
Infrared Thermal Fusion
A thermal sensor layer independently verifies human presence, adding a second layer of confirmation to every detection event — so alerts are grounded in converging evidence, not a single data source.
encrypted
Secure Connectivity
All data is transmitted with end-to-end encryption over WiFi, with automatic 4G LTE failover. On-device edge processing ensures sensitive health data is processed locally first — minimizing what leaves the home.
dashboard_customize
Dashboard & Alerts
A unified caregiver dashboard with live room status, trend charts, and tiered notification routing — designed for both family members and professional care teams to stay informed without feeling overwhelmed.
