How we build

Small on purpose.

Health apps are usually overwhelming. Ours aren't.

target

One job, done well

We leave features out. Every app answers a single question a real family asks, and answers it fast.

menu_book

Sourced, never invented

Anything clinical is grounded in a published guideline and cited in the app. No made-up thresholds.

spa

Calm by design

Warm, readable and quiet in design, making it easy to use one-handed at 3 a.m. and never phrased like a verdict.
