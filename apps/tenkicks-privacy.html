TenKicks Privacy Policy

Effective September 21, 2026 · eLumino (“we”, “us”)

TenKicks is a kick counter for pregnancy. This policy explains what the app stores, where it lives, and the choices you have. In short: your kick counts stay on your device, there is no account, and we do not receive your information.

What TenKicks stores

InformationWhy
Your kick-count sessions: start and end times, and each movement’s time and intensity (light, medium or strong)To show your counts, history and reports.
Your settings: target count, time window, and reminder timeTo run the app the way you set it up.

All of this is saved on your device only. We have no servers that receive it, and we cannot see it.

We do not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone audio or advertising ID. TenKicks does not show ads, we do not sell information, and the app does not use third-party analytics or advertising tools.

Optional features

  • Reminders: if you turn on the daily reminder, TenKicks schedules a notification on your device. It is created and delivered locally; nothing is sent to us.
  • Export: if you export your data (CSV or PDF), the file is created on your device and goes only where you choose to share it.
  • iPhone iCloud sync (iOS only): if you turn it on, your data is copied to your own private iCloud storage, managed by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy. We cannot access it.
  • Device backup: your phone’s own backup (for example Android Auto Backup to your Google account, or iCloud Backup) may include TenKicks’s data, under your device maker’s privacy policy and your backup settings.
  • TenKicks Pro (one-time purchase): purchases are processed by Google Play or the App Store. We do not see or store your payment details. The app only checks with the store whether you own Pro.

Your choices

  • Delete your data: Settings → Delete data removes all sessions and kicks from your device. Uninstalling the app also removes its data from your device.
  • Export your data: use the export option in Reports to get a copy.
  • Reminders: turn them off in Settings, or in your phone’s notification settings.

How long we keep it

We do not keep any of your TenKicks data, because it never leaves your device through us. Data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app.

Not medical advice

TenKicks supports the widely used practice of counting fetal movements but does not diagnose anything and does not replace your care provider. If movement slows or changes, or you are ever concerned, contact your care provider right away.

Children

TenKicks is for adults. It is not directed at children.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way (for example, adding an account, cloud storage, advertising or analytics), we will update the date above and tell you in the app before the change takes effect, and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

Contact

eLumino, Canada · hello@elumino.ca. If we cannot resolve a concern, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
